# Privacy Policy

**Logbook — Mileage Tracker**
Last updated: May 1, 2026

## Overview

Logbook is a mileage tracking app for iOS. This privacy policy explains how the app handles your data.

## Data Collection

Logbook does not collect, transmit, or share any personal data. All information you enter into the app — including trip records, odometer readings, destinations, and settings — is stored locally on your device only.

## Location Data

Logbook requests access to your device's location only when GPS tracking mode is active during a trip. Location data is used solely to calculate trip distance. It is never stored beyond the trip record, never transmitted off your device, and never shared with any third party.

## Data Storage

All trip data is stored locally on your device using AsyncStorage. Logbook has no backend server and no user accounts. Your data never leaves your device unless you choose to export it manually via the CSV export feature.

## Third-Party Services

Logbook does not use any third-party analytics, advertising, or tracking services.

## Data Deletion

You can delete any trip record at any time within the app. Uninstalling the app removes all associated data from your device.
